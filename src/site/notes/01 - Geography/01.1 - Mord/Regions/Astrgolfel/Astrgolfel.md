# <span style="font-family: 'Cinzel Decorative';font-size: 36pt">Astrgolfel</span>
 <span style="font-family: 'Cinzel Decorative';font-size: 16pt">Where the star Gol fell</span>
>*"There are few stars where we're going..."*
>*— [[Eygon Van Grimm\|Eygon Van Grimm]], vampire hunter*
![Pasted image 20260604201907.png](/img/user/00%20-%20Wiki/Utilities/Images/Pasted%20image%2020260604201907.png)

Astrgolfel (pronounced: /a-STER-goel-fehl/) is an old name in the Duanish tongue that caught on during the [[Age of the Horizon.\|Age of the Horizon.]] Translated literally, it means, "Where the star [[Gol\|Gol]] Fell." The region today is much more commonly referred to, collectively, as "The Gols".
# Overview
Astrgolfel is a bleak region dominated by vampiric overlords who rule during the night. The land is subject to the influence of the Shadowfell more so than any other.
## Hallmarks
Scattered city states, vampires, gnomes, the Shadowfell, artifice, and invention
## Three Interesting Facts
- Veils into the Shadowfell are present all throughout Astrgolfel, along with invading monsters from Gol.
- Astrgolfel is called home by infamous artificers and inventors. They live as hermits on the edge of sanity, creating advanced technology the likes not seen anywhere else.
- Angelic churches, though popular and widespread, are outlawed by the vampiric overlords who rule the region.
# Characters from Astrgolfel
## A Somber Few.
Astrgolfel is not a happy place. At the best of times, it is safe and protected, but it is not a land of good fortune. The people of Astrgolfel often carry heavy burdens in their hearts; the vampiric presence, hobgoblin raids, monster attacks, and war between nations has wreaked havoc for families and communities. Consider what your character may have lost in life, and how it may have affected them.
## Hopeful Despite it All.
Life is itself a blessing, and so long as one lives, they are blessed. Despite their many hardships, the people of Astrgolfel find hope to go on. They are a willful people, with a determination not found in many others. They come together during hard times, and find warmth and comfort in unexpected places; in their outlawed churches, during hidden weddings, and with whoever they can find close to call family. Where does your character find hope?
## Shadow-touched.
Hexes and infections spread from the seeping veils to Gol. The Shadowfell taints the land, and the people as well. Your character may be touched by the Shadowfell's presence, a fell omen on their future. Your character may be haunted, doomed with bad luck, or even stricken with vampirism or worse! What secrets are you hiding? What wounds are you not willing to show on your neck?
# Contemplation and Meditation
The name "Astrgolfel" has lasted thousands of years thanks to the immortal lords who keep it alive. Most people these days refer to the region as "the Gols." Indeed, few stars may be seen through the dull fog that pervades the region save for the Dark Star [[Shadowfell\|Gol]]. It sheds an antilight, a negative brightness so intense it punches through even dense smoke or mists. Monsters grow old and powerful in this treacherous wilderness, and the sun never seems to shine bright enough to pierce the overcast skies. It rains so commonly that farmers are known to pray for periods of drought.
Unfortunately for them, the sun fears the region of Astrgolfel. The region has been plagued by vampires for as long as anyone alive might remember thanks to the [[Shadowfell\|Shadowfell's]] influence. [[05 - Legendarium/05.1 - Characters/Night Lord\|King Volgar Leofric]], or the Night Lord to his sycophants, has prevailed over his kingdom of [[01 - Geography/01.1 - Mord/Regions/Astrgolfel/Realms/Dunland\|Dunland]] for the last nine-hundred years. His power in the region, whereas it has fluctuated in the past while battling competing vampires, has reached its historical apex. The Dunlanders have complete dominion all throughout the mountain range known as [[The Grey\|The Grey]]. Other vampiric overlords preside throughout Astrgolfel as well, but none such as mighty as the Night Lord. In every vampire-controlled realm, worship of the Angelic churches is forbidden and illegal, especially the [[Church of the Holy Avenger\|Church of the Holy Avenger]], whose mission is to put the fiends back into the coffins from which they rose. 
Somewhat paradoxically, territories controlled by vampires tend to be safer against monstrous threats thanks to these overlords. The mighty [[01 - Geography/01.1 - Mord/Regions/Astrgolfel/Realms/Augustine\|Augustine]], built around the dynasty of the first vampire, [[05 - Legendarium/05.1 - Characters/Count August the Red\|Count August the Red]], is perhaps the greatest in sheer might, but lacks in real infrastructure or governing ability. The people of Augustine are ruled mostly by terror and terror alone. In contrast, the city-state of [[01 - Geography/01.1 - Mord/Regions/Astrgolfel/Realms/Golz\|Golz]] is ruled by one [[Princess Lucy the Invincible\|Princess Lucy the Invincible]], who began a cult-religion centered around herself, granting thralldom to her most-loyal minions. Though her rule is tyrannical, she endeavors to isolate her flock from any would-be competitors, and thus endeavors to maintain living conditions that keep her citizens content. Though it may be safer than the harsh wilderness, life in the vampire-kingdoms is dominated by the singular truth: that the rulers view mortals as livestock to be bred, nurtured, and then slaughtered.
Outside of the immortal governments of vampires lie scattered city states and small villages relying only on their wits to survive. The kingdoms of [[01 - Geography/01.1 - Mord/Regions/Astrgolfel/Realms/Grisholm\|Grisholm]] and [[Grissonant \|Grissonant ]]for example, are ruled from their capitals in old elven ruins made by the [[Gris\|Gris]] of [[01 - Geography/01.1 - Mord/Regions/Astrgolfel/Realms/The Dark Houses\|The Dark Houses]]. [[01 - Geography/01.1 - Mord/Regions/Astrgolfel/Realms/The Empire of Borboun\|The Empire of Borboun]] and the kingdoms of [[01 - Geography/01.1 - Mord/Regions/Astrgolfel/Realms/Soleil\|Soleil]] and [[01 - Geography/01.1 - Mord/Regions/Astrgolfel/Realms/Delune\|Delune]] are all mostly composed of humans and halflings. These kingdoms have bloody histories of war and conflict over the past two hundred years, but also rich cultural exchanges and trade networks across the [[Sea of Areppo\|Sea of Areppo]].
In the more isolated hamlets are the remote artificers of Astrgolfel. Small and informal communities of these inventors live around the region, drawn to the un-light of Gol and the strange energies of the Shadowfell. The most well-known of these ascetics is the scientist, [[Dr. Quincy Morrison\|Dr. Quincy Morrison]], who is said to live inside a great bronze golem, a mobile castle of belching furnaces and steam boilers. Some towns in Astrgolfel have been graced by the presence of such travelling craftsmen, and have been subsequently equipped with tangled networks of copper pipes and wires, while others are protected by automatons built for battle. Other experimenters, of a darker enterprise, delve into the depths of the occult, losing their sanity in exchange for necromantic secrets. It is said that the ritual to create a [[Remnant\|Remnant]] was first performed in the dark of Astrgolfel.
And yet, even the mightiest Morrison automaton cannot prevent all of the death that suffuses Astrgolfel. The Hobgoblins of [[Thorne\|Thorne]], are powerful enemies to both mortals and the vampires. Their host is mainly congregated throughout the islands known as the [[Western Gols\|Western Gols]]. From their ports they launch raiding parties and pirate crews to terrorize the Western coasts from the [[Alabaster Sea\|Alabaster Sea]] to the north, all the way to the [[Zephyr Sea\|Zephyr Sea]] to the south.
But all is not lost for the people of Astrgolfel. Great nations, such as [[01 - Geography/01.1 - Mord/Regions/Astrgolfel/Realms/Dane's Halls\|Dane's Halls]], [[01 - Geography/01.1 - Mord/Regions/Astrgolfel/Realms/The Holy Empire of Grimm\|The Holy Empire of Grimm]], [[01 - Geography/01.1 - Mord/Regions/Astrgolfel/Realms/Myradan\|Myradan]], or [[01 - Geography/01.1 - Mord/Regions/Astrgolfel/Realms/Vance\|Vance]], are chock-full of Dwarvish protectors, Elvish and Gnomish scholars on the Shadowfell, and thousands of fetchers just waiting to get their hands on the glory and fortune waiting to be won from the hands of villainy.

# Geography
Astrgolfel is a temperate peninsula on the western coast of [[01 - Geography/01.1 - Mord/Mord\|Mord]] bordering [[The Bellatorian Ocean\|The Bellatorian Ocean]] to the west, and the [[Erdlands\|Erdlands]] to the east. The region is home to an old mountain range called [[The Grey\|The Grey]], and a series of islands off its west coast called the [[Western Gols\|Western Gols]]. The region borders the [[Sea of Areppo\|Sea of Areppo]] on its southern coasts, and the [[Alabaster Sea\|Alabaster Sea]] to the north.
Astrgolfel is known for its overcast weather and a distinct lack of sunshine. It is believed that this fell curse was placed on the land long ago, when the dark star [[Shadowfell\|Gol]] came to hang in the sky above Astrgolfel. Nevertheless, the region is favored by its diverse landscape of temperate, forested mountains and hills alongside rolling grasslands and fertile ground.
